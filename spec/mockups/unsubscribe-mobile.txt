Notes:
- Works without signing in; the token in the link identifies the user.
- After clicking Unsubscribe (a POST), the box reads "You're unsubscribed /
  We won't email you@example.com about new deck recommendations."
- If the token is unknown or older than 30 days, the box reads "This link
  has expired / Unsubscribe links work for 30 days. Sign in to manage your
  email settings instead." (links to Settings).
- Desktop uses the same content at full width.
